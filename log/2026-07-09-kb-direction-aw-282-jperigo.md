# Knowledge-Base Direction — AW-238 Held Open for a Deliberate Structure Decision (AW-282)

## Context

Confluence came online through the Rovo integration (2026-07-09; see the same-day convention-change entry for the Atlassian-wide no-diary-references ruling). A review of the DSKB space found Victoria had already stood up real structure — an 11-section Model Documentation Template, a Governance & Maintenance page (ownership model, update-at-point-of-change triggers, 90-day staleness rule, label conventions, decision-log standard), a Client Onboarding page, a seeded JDS MMM page, and an InsightCore draft. Against AW-238's success criteria (platform chosen, template exists, KB seeded with one model), the epic is complete; its only story (AW-242) is Done.

## What Happened / Decided

- **AW-238 stays In Progress by owner choice** — not closed on its stood-up success criteria.
- **AW-282 created** — `[Spike] Define the DSKB knowledge-base structure and documentation standard`, assigned to Victoria, Backlog. It gives her the question (audiences and their recurring questions → page-type inventory with creation triggers → supply- vs demand-driven generation → sustainable minimum publish standard → where runbooks live relative to AW-245/AW-246), maps every existing DSKB page to keep/revise/park, and ends with the Governance page updated and the structure published on the DSKB home page.
- **Owner steer recorded:** the team is not to be held to the current template-first structure — "we are not really using the tool at the moment." Near-zero usage means structure is cheap to change now and expensive after pages accumulate; the current shape is one candidate, not the default.

## Rationale

AW-238 measured *standing the KB up*, and that succeeded — but nothing on the board owned *adoption*, which is why the KB feels unused despite being well-built. Rather than close the epic on artifacts-exist and let structure ossify by inertia, the spike converts the implicit template-first standard into an explicit, owner-reviewed decision while it is still cheap. The spike deliberately does not pre-decide the answer: Victoria built the current structure days ago and owns the epic, so the story hands her the constraint (usage-driven evidence, team-size-sustainable maintenance) and the decision, not a conclusion. Candidate demand-driven page types surfaced in the discussion — portfolio one-pager, per-client results-interpretation guides, operating runbooks, integration contracts — are recorded in the spike's questions as options to weigh, not commitments.

## Follow-ups

- Victoria runs AW-282; the structure decision is reviewed with the owner before publishing.
- AW-238 closes when AW-282's decided standard is published (the epic then ends on a deliberate standard rather than an inherited one).
- Documentation stories elsewhere on the board (AW-240 WAB inputs, AW-241 JDS inputs) should target whatever structure AW-282 decides.
